# Interview notes — Myntra wishlist (primary research)

Segment: metro working women 24–32. Full write-ups: /research.

Screener: woman 24–32, metro (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Coimbatore, plus Mumbai/Pune in the rooms), Myntra at least weekly, 15+ wishlisted items, mix of workwear and occasion, delayed a saved purchase in the last 60 days.

Respondents: Ananya Rao (Bengaluru), Riya Mehta (Mumbai), Meera Iyer (Delhi), Priya Deshpande (Pune), Kavya Reddy (Hyderabad), Sneha Kapoor (Gurugram).

Each write-up answers every Part 3 prompt:

1. Why they saved each item
2. Whether they still intend to purchase it
3. What is stopping them
4. What would make them purchase it
5. What information they still need
6. Whether they are considering alternatives
7. What happens outside the app before they decide
8. How they currently overcome uncertainty

Locked problem: fit uncertainty at wishlist revisit + return/seal-tag fear. Bookmark-only users are quarantined, not converted. Sale-wait named and disqualified.
